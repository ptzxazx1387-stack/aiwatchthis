in practice

Three principles carry it all

Breathe

Whitespace is the material. Sections rest for 9–12 vertical units; content never crowds the void.

Whisper

Light weights, muted ink, soft glows. Hierarchy through size and space — never through loudness.

Drift

Everything moves as if weightless — slow entrances, gentle lifts, orbs that never land. Motion that snaps is motion that breaks the spell.
